Abstract
  • • Background: Campus placement cycles represent high-concurrency recruitment operations engaging students, company recruiters, and institutional Training & Placement Officers (TPOs).
  • • The Problem: Traditional workflows rely on scattered Google Forms, manual spreadsheet trackers, and unproctored assessment links, creating severe communication bottlenecks and fraud risks.
  • • SmartHire Solution: A unified cloud recruitment ecosystem pairing role-based placement tracking with client-side proctoring, multi-language coding sandboxes, and transparent career readiness modules.
Introduction
  • • Tripartite Stakeholders: Unifies Students (drive tracking & skill practice), Recruiters (requisitions & candidate reviews), and TPOs (compliance auditing).
  • • Proctored Assessments: Ingests 32 corporate mock OA suites (86 verified problems) with client-side anti-cheat telemetry (tab switch, face presence, clipboard locking).
  • • Ethical Human-in-the-Loop Gate: Unlike black-box AI platforms that auto-reject candidates, SmartHire isolates ATS heuristic scoring strictly as formative guidance for students.
