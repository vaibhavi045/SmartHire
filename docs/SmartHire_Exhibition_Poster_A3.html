Abstract
  • • Background: College campus placement drives require intense coordination between student applicants, company recruiters, and the Training & Placement Office (TPO). Traditional hiring drives rely on Google Forms, emails, and spreadsheet trackers, leading to manual sorting delays, lack of candidate feedback, and disorganized records across academic departments.
  • • The Problem: Manual workflows lack automated test proctoring, require third-party paid external links for coding evaluations, and provide students with zero pre-application feedback on how well their resume matches the recruiter's specific job description (JD).
  • • SmartHire Solution: An end-to-end web and mobile-assisted on-campus recruitment and preparation platform built with React.js, Node.js, Express, and Supabase PostgreSQL. It unifies job posting, student eligibility screening, online coding tests via JDoodle, browser webcam proctoring, instant ATS resume scoring, and mobile phone push notifications into a single integrated system.
  • • Project Objective & Scope: To replace manual campus placement coordination with an automated, cheat-resistant recruitment and preparation ecosystem featuring verified student KYC, cloud sandboxed coding exams, and direct mobile push notifications.
Introduction
  • • Three Role-Based Portals: SmartHire provides three separate interfaces: Student Portal & App (job applications, push alerts on phone, coding tests, ATS score check), Recruiter Portal (post jobs, review applicants, advance stages), and College Admin Portal (verify students, approve drives, track placement statistics).
  • • Online Test Proctoring: Includes assessments with webcam face detection (using face-api.js), tab-switch warnings, and fullscreen enforcement to ensure fair evaluation without demanding heavy media streaming servers.
  • • Integrated Code Runner: Connects to the JDoodle API to compile and execute student programs in C++, Java, Python, and JavaScript against automated test cases with runtime and memory metrics.

Conclusion & Future Scope
  • • Conclusion: SmartHire successfully replaces scattered spreadsheets and manual placement coordination with a unified, modern on-campus recruitment and preparation system. It bridges students, recruiters, and the college placement cell through verified profiles, transparent application stages, built-in coding tests, and proctoring.
  • • Future Scope 1 (Student Mobile App & Push Notifications): Developing a dedicated cross-platform mobile application so students receive real-time push notifications on their phones for new drive postings, exam schedules, and round-by-round shortlist results.
  • • Future Scope 2 (AI Voice Mock Interviews): Integrating an LLM-assisted conversational voice mock interview practice module to help students prepare for behavioral and technical interviews.
  • • Future Scope 3 (NIRF & NAAC Analytics): Providing automated placement analytics reporting, statistical salary distributions, and instant compliance data exports.
